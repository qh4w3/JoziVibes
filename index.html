Explore Johannesburg's Best

A survey was conducted and 77 responses were recieved.All respondents were female, and from the OWLAG community. But each one had a different opinion on where they would want to go as a first#1 choice. This was based on their upcoming grade trips; majority was excited while the minority was unsure about how to feel as this would be their first grade trip. Therefore based on our survey of 77 locals, here’s what people love most

📊 Survey Results: Favorite Attraction

23% voted Museum of Illusions - "Illusion Activites"

71% voted Gold Reef City - "Theme Park Rides"

6% voted Elephant Sanctuary - "Group Activities"

📊 Survey Results: Favorite Activity Type

Adventure: Rollercoasters & rides won

Relaxation: Animal interactions none mentioned

Social: Interactive exhibits won
